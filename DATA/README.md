### Related CVE(s): ARAŞTIRILMADI

This category covers weaknesses related to how an application stores, handles, and safeguards information. It includes areas such as cryptography misuse, information leakage, and unsafe file upload mechanisms, all of which can undermine the confidentiality or integrity of sensitive data. Failures in this category typically arise when data is exposed, processed insecurely, or left unprotected against unauthorized access or manipulation.
